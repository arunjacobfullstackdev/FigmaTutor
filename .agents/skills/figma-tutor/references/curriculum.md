# Figma Learning Curriculum

Use this roadmap to guide the user sequentially or let them jump straight into
any module based on their immediate goal.

## Level 1: Foundations & Canvas Fluency
1. **Interface & Navigation**: Canvas, layers panel, properties panel, zooming, panning, nudging (`Shift + Arrow`), and outline mode (`Command + Y`).
2. **Frames vs. Groups vs. Sections**: Why Frames are the building block of UI and logos, clip content, and organizing pages with Sections.
3. **Shapes, Alignment & Constraints**: Primitives, tidy up, smart selection, and responsive Constraints inside non-Auto-Layout frames.
4. **Typography & Color**: Text resizing modes (Auto Width, Auto Height, Fixed Size), fills/strokes, opacity vs. layer opacity, and gradients.

## Level 2: Vector & Logo Craft
5. **Vector Networks & Pen Tool**: Nodes, Bézier handles, bending, joining paths, and how Figma's Vector Networks differ from traditional single-path tools.
6. **Boolean Operations & Flattening**: Union, Subtract, Intersect, Exclude, non-destructive editing, Outline Stroke, and clean SVG export.
7. **Masks & Effects**: Alpha/vector/luminance masks, drop shadows, layer blur, background blur, and export settings (SVG, PNG @2x/@3x, PDF).

## Level 3: Responsive Layout Mastery
8. **Auto Layout Fundamentals**: Direction, gap, padding, alignment, and absolute positioning (`Ignore Auto Layout`).
9. **Resizing Behavior**: Hug Contents, Fill Container, Fixed Width/Height, and Min/Max width/height constraints.
10. **Nested Auto Layout & Wrap**: Building responsive cards, navbars, forms, and wrapping grids without brittle hacks.

## Level 4: Reusable Systems
11. **Components & Instances**: Main components, overrides, resetting overrides, and instance swapping.
12. **Component Properties & Variants**: Variant sets, Boolean properties, Text properties, Instance Swap properties, and preferred values.
13. **Styles vs. Variables (Design Tokens)**: Color, Number, String, and Boolean variables; primitive vs. semantic tokens; modes (Light/Dark, Compact/Comfortable).
14. **Design Libraries & Governance**: Naming conventions (`Category/Component/State`), publishing libraries, and maintaining consistency.

## Level 5: Prototyping, Collaboration & Production Handoff
15. **Interactive Prototyping**: Flows, triggers, Smart Animate, overlays, scrolling areas, and interactive components.
16. **Dev Mode & Handoff**: Annotations, ready-for-dev status, inspecting CSS/tokens, Code Connect, and exporting production assets.
17. **Figma + AI / MCP Workflows**: Inspecting designs programmatically, generating frames with `use_figma`, and bridging design to code.
